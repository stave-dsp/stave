stave 0.6.0 (unreleased)

New: Audio Units (FRAMEWORK.md F-AU). `stave::export!` also makes the crate an Audio Unit v2 on macOS (`scripts/bundle-au.sh`, `install.sh` and `validate-formats.sh au`; auval in CI; `plugin_checks!` runs the validation suite through each plugin's Audio Unit). `Info` gains `au: Option<AuCodes>`, the Audio Unit's manufacturer and subtype codes (`None` derives them from `vendor` and `id`); `stave::identity` reports them as `au`. `examples/pluck` is the example instrument.

Migrating a plugin
- An `Info` written without `..Info::EFFECT` (or `..Info::INSTRUMENT`) → add `au: None,` (or pin the codes: `au: Some(AuCodes::new(*b"Exmp", *b"tilt"))`)
