# notes: typical values only. The LM741C rows give slew rate 0.5 V/us (unity gain) and transient response rise time 0.3 us (unity gain), but no bandwidth; the gbw row is the sheet's own conversion, note (2): BW (MHz) = 0.35 / rise time (us) = 1.167 MHz. The LM741A table prints that bandwidth row as 1.5 MHz typical, 0.437 MHz minimum. The rise time's 5% overshoot shows a second pole the one-pole macromodel leaves out.
